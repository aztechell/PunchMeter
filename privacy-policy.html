PunchMeter Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

PunchMeter is a standalone Wear OS app that uses your watch's motion sensors to estimate punch speed, acceleration, punch type, and game scores. The app processes this information locally on your watch. It does not require an account or automatically send your results to the developer.

1. Developer and Contact

PunchMeter is developed by Aztechell. For privacy questions, contact aztechell@gmail.com.

2. Information Processed on Your Watch

PunchMeter reads motion data from available sensors, including the accelerometer, gyroscope, linear acceleration sensor, and rotation sensors. These readings are used to detect punches and calculate the displayed measurements and scores.

The app stores the following information in its local app storage:

  • Recent results: the last ten accepted punches, including available speed and acceleration estimates, punch type, game score, result quality, time, selected wrist and stance, and information identifying the calculation version.
  • Best results and preferences: records, the current result or its hidden state, selected mode, speed units, wrist, stance, and preferences such as sound, vibration, and display options.
  • Technical device and sensor information: device model and manufacturer, Android and app version information, sensor names, vendors, capabilities, and sampling information used to understand the watch's sensor limits.

The app may check system audio settings and playback status to respect volume, mute, Do Not Disturb, and audio output choices when playing its local sounds. It does not record audio.

3. How the App Uses and Shares Information

Motion readings and locally stored information are used to provide measurements, game feedback, history, best results, and your chosen settings. Technical information supports sensor operation and troubleshooting.

PunchMeter has no advertising, analytics SDKs, developer-operated cloud service, or automatic upload of results or diagnostic recordings. It does not sell your information. The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, heart rate, or Health Connect data.

The app requests access to high sampling rates for motion sensors and permission to vibrate for feedback. These permissions do not give the developer remote access to your watch or its data.

4. Diagnostic Recordings in Test Versions

Engineering and test versions may let you manually start and stop a diagnostic recording. A recording can include raw motion sensor samples, timing, device and sensor information, and calculated results. These files are saved locally and are not automatically sent to the developer.

If you choose to provide a recording for support or testing, it will be used to investigate the issue or improve punch detection and measurements. Only share recordings or other information you want the developer to receive. Clearing history or best results does not delete these separate diagnostic files.

5. Storage, Retention, and Deletion

The recent punch list is limited to ten results; older entries are replaced as new results are saved. Best results, preferences, and other local files remain until they are reset, replaced, or removed.

  • Clear history removes recent punch history and the current result. It preserves best results and settings.
  • Clear best resets best results. It preserves punch history and settings.
  • Tapping the displayed result to hide it does not delete its history entry or any best result.
  • Clearing the app's storage through system settings, where available, or uninstalling the app removes its local app data, including diagnostic files stored there.

Removing local app data does not necessarily remove system backups or copies you have exported or shared. Those copies are controlled separately.

6. System Backup and Restore

PunchMeter's current configuration allows Android system backup. Depending on your watch, operating system, account provider, and backup settings, some app data may be included in system backups or transferred when you restore or move to another device. This is separate from any service operated by the developer.

Backup availability, storage, retention, restoration, and deletion are controlled by the relevant platform or backup provider and your settings. Uninstalling PunchMeter does not guarantee deletion of backup copies. See Android's backup documentation and your device or backup provider's information for details.

7. Security

PunchMeter uses the platform's app-specific storage and access controls to protect local data. Protection also depends on your watch's lock, operating system, updates, and backup settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Information You Choose to Send by Email

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request, provide support, and investigate reported issues. It may be retained as needed for those purposes; you may contact the developer with a request to delete correspondence.

Email is handled through the services used by you and the developer, under those providers' own privacy policies. Do not include passwords or information that is not needed for your request.

9. This Website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no JavaScript, analytics, external fonts, or tracking tools added by the developer. Visiting this website is separate from using the PunchMeter app.

GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation. GitHub handles website visitor information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when PunchMeter's behavior or relevant privacy requirements change. The current policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
